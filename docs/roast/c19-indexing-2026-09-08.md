## Phase 3: the adversarial pass

**Silent drops.** Rows 4, 15, 19, 20, 23 and 26 were absent from the first
report entirely: six requirements the report implied were covered. This is the
exact failure the gate exists to catch, and it caught it after the merge rather
than before, which is the honest thing to record.

**Interpretation drift.** Two, both mine. C19.5 says "every URL the platform has
EVER published"; I substituted "every URL currently in the sitemap", the easier
set and the one I already had. C19.7 says "one community page with events"; I
substituted "one templated page above the threshold" because no community page
qualified, and reported the substitute without naming it.

**Match versus surpass.** The brief does not use surpass here. Not applicable.

**Unverifiable claims.** "Structured data added and validated" was the claim and
nothing tested it. Testing it found two real defects. Every other claim in the
report is now backed by a named artefact under `EVIDENCE\C19`.

**The generic test.** The policy, the threshold and the checks are built on this
platform's own route tree, taxonomy and sitemap. Nothing here fits another
product.

**AI-tell sweep.** Zero, over 956 files, by the copy gate in the pre-push gate.

**Regression sweep (DESIGN-LOCK).** Three visible changes, all of them asked for
by the brief rather than done for tidiness, and each captured at 390, 768 and
1440: a "Faith and worship" section on `/communities` (C19.1's reachability
question); the city descriptor in the `/events/browse/[city]` hero (C19.4's
"copy that reflects that specific city"); a link on the venue name on the event
page and one on the city page (reachability). No hero height, colour, spacing
token or layout was touched. axe-core zero at 390 and 1440 on every one.

**Founder-cost test.** One step is his and it is a judgement, not a task:
confirm the threshold. Resubmitting the sitemap in Search Console genuinely
needs his session and is stated as impossible for a machine here.

**Evidence-visibility test.** Captures at three viewports for eleven surfaces;
every table, sweep and check output is a file at a named path.

Finishing the six turned up SIX MORE DEFECTS the first pass had shipped, every
one of them fixed:

1. 488 of the 550 published URLs emitted an ItemList with `itemListElement: []`:
   markup asserting a list and listing nothing, on exactly the empty pages C19.3

   join came back null. The node is omitted rather than empty.

   asserted "never to another redirect" was blind to it because the second hop
   lives in a different module. Both fixed.
4. Five `/faith/[faith]` pages nothing on the platform linked to, on an index
   page whose own subheading said they were browseable.
5. Twenty-one `/events/browse/[city]` pages nothing linked to; the component
   written for exactly that, `CityRailTile`, was never rendered anywhere.
6. Every `/venues/[handle]` profile unlinked, including from the event page that
   prints the venue's name.

## Phase 5: decision evidence

The one decision here is the indexing threshold, and it is the owner's.
Competitor: not applicable, no incumbent publishes a threshold. Market: Google's
duplicate-collapsing behaviour is the constraint and is why the clause exists.
Our code: `DISCOVERY_INDEXING_THRESHOLD`, one constant, read by the pages and by
the sitemap. Test plan: watch indexed pages against published pages in Search
Console; if a page holding three events still fails to index, the threshold is
not the lever and the copy is. Put to the owner in REVIEW-QUEUE.md.
